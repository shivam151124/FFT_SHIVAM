Building the people who will build what comes next.

The curriculum builds conceptual depth and practical proficiency across programming, algorithms, database systems, cloud computing, and software engineering.

02 / ABOUT DEPARTMENT

About the Department

VISION

To be recognised as a centre of excellence in computer applications education, producing graduates who apply computing skills responsibly to solve real problems across industry and society.

MISSION

  • Deliver a curriculum that balances theory with hands-on practice.
  • Build partnerships with industry for internships and live projects.
  • Encourage research, innovation, and ethical use of technology.
  • Support every student's placement and higher-study goals.

OBJECTIVES

  • Strengthen core programming and problem-solving ability.
  • Provide specialisation tracks matched to current industry demand.
  • Develop communication, teamwork, and project-management skills.
  • Prepare students for certification and competitive examinations.
